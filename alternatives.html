3. Control Dock

Left rail of six favourite apps, right side a dashboard: clock, weather, cluster/home status tiles, then an app grid. Utilitarian, fast to hit with a d-pad, room for live data. Middle effort: Projectivy covers rail plus grid plus hardware monitor; the status tiles need a custom launcher or a widget-capable one.
